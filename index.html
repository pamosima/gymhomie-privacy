GymHomie Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

GymHomie is an iPhone and Apple Watch app for following a gym training plan. This policy explains which data the app uses and where it goes. In short: your data stays on your devices, and it is only shared with Apple Health, Strava and Spotify when you choose to use them.

Data stored on your devices

Your training plans, exercises, trainings, sets, ratings, notes and body measurements are stored only on your iPhone. GymHomie has no account, no server of its own, and does not upload this data anywhere except as described below. Deleting the app deletes this data.

Apple Health

With your permission, GymHomie reads workouts, heart rate, active and resting calories, walking and running distance, weight and waist circumference from Apple Health. It uses them to show your heart rate during a training, tick off cardio, import strength workouts and chart your body progress. With your permission, it saves your finished trainings to Apple Health as workouts, with their heart rate, calories and distance.

Data from Apple Health is never used for advertising or marketing, never sold, and never shared with third parties, except when you upload a training to Strava as described below. You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.

Strava (optional)

If you connect your Strava account, GymHomie uploads your finished trainings to Strava as activities. An upload contains the plan name, the start time and duration, the exercises with their done sets (weight, reps or seconds), the warm-up, cardio and cool-down summary, and – if available in Apple Health – the heart rate and active calories measured during the training. Your training notes are not uploaded.

Who can see these activities follows your privacy settings on Strava. The sign-in tokens for Strava are stored in the iPhone's Keychain. Disconnecting Strava in GymHomie's settings revokes the app's access and deletes the tokens. Activities already uploaded stay on Strava until you delete them there. Strava's handling of your data is described in the Strava Privacy Policy.

Spotify (optional)

If you connect Spotify, GymHomie controls playback in the Spotify app on your iPhone: it starts the playlist you chose when a training starts and pauses it at the end. To show the current track on your Apple Watch, the app receives the track title and artist from the Spotify app. To list your playlists it reads their names. Nothing about your trainings is sent to Spotify. The sign-in tokens are stored in the iPhone's Keychain and deleted when you disconnect. Spotify's handling of your data is described in the Spotify Privacy Policy.

Apple Watch

The iPhone and the Apple Watch app exchange the state of the running training (phase, heart rate, rest timer, current exercise and track) directly between your devices.

No tracking, no analytics, no ads

GymHomie contains no advertising, no analytics or tracking libraries, and does not track you across apps or websites. If you share diagnostics with Apple, Apple may provide anonymous crash reports to the developer, as described in Apple's privacy policy.

Children

GymHomie is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

Because your data is stored on your devices, you can view, change and delete it in the app at any time. For data uploaded to Strava, use Strava to view or delete it. You can also contact the developer with any privacy question or request.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
